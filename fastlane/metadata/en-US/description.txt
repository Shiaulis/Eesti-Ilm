Check Estonia's weather forecast in seconds—clean, fast, and completely free of ads or tracking.

Eesti Ilm brings you day and night forecasts from the Estonian Environment Agency (ilmateenistus.ee), the national weather service of Estonia, in a native iOS app built for clarity.

What you get:
• Day & night forecasts for Estonia powered by ilmateenistus.ee
• Clear temperature ranges, wind conditions, and weather descriptions
• Small and medium Home Screen widgets to see the forecast at a glance
• English and Estonian language support
• Privacy-first by design: no account, no ads, and no personal data collected

Whether you live in Estonia or are planning a trip to Tallinn, Tartu, or the Baltic coast, get reliable local forecasts without the clutter.

Eesti Ilm is an independent app and is not affiliated with the Estonian Environment Agency.
