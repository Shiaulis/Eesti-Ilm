Eesti Ilm gives you instant access to the weather forecast for Estonia in a simple, clean app.

Forecasts come from the weather service of the Estonian Environment Agency (ilmateenistus.ee), the official source of weather information in Estonia.

Features:
• Weather forecasts for Estonia from the national weather service
• Home Screen widget in small and medium sizes to check the weather at a glance
• No account, no ads, no personal data collected

Eesti Ilm is an independent app and is not affiliated with the Estonian Environment Agency.
